# Welcome to fixin.

Point your camera at a problem on your car, home or boat and get a likely
diagnosis in seconds — analysed on your phone, so it works without a signal.

Every diagnosis comes with a repair guide for that exact fault: what to check,
the tools you need, safety warnings and a time estimate. Over 2,200 guides.

Save to history, group repairs into projects, and work through them as a
step-by-step checklist.
